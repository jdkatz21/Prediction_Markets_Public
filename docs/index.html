Kalshi-Implied Macroeconomic Expectations

Data supporting "Kalshi and The Rise of Macro Markets" (Diercks, Katz, Wright 2026)

This website provides daily trade-level, probability distribution, and moments data for Kalshi-implied expectations on the Federal Funds Rate and other macroeconomic releases.

Data updates every morning (around 6am EST) with previous day's trades (days are in UTC).

Please cite:
Diercks, A., Katz, J., & Wright, J. (2026). Kalshi and The Rise of Macro Markets. NBER Working Paper No. 34702
